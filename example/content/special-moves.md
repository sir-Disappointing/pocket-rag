# Special moves

## Castling
<!-- keys: O-O, O-O-O, 0-0, 0-0-0 -->
Castling is a single move with the king and a rook. The king moves two squares towards the rook, and the rook jumps over to the square the king crossed. Castling kingside is written O-O, queenside O-O-O.

You may only castle if all of these are true:
- neither the king nor that rook has moved before in the game;
- there are no pieces between the king and the rook;
- the king is not in check;
- the king does not pass through or land on a square that is attacked.

The rook may be attacked, and on the queenside the rook may pass an attacked square (b1 or b8).

## En passant
<!-- keys: e.p. -->
En passant is a special pawn capture. When a pawn moves two squares from its starting square and lands right next to an opposing pawn, that opposing pawn may capture it as if it had moved only one square. The capture is made diagonally onto the square the pawn passed over. It must be done immediately on the next move, or the right is lost.

## Promotion
When a pawn reaches the last rank (rank 8 for White, rank 1 for Black), it must be exchanged for a queen, rook, bishop or knight of the same colour, as part of the same move. You choose, regardless of which pieces are still on the board, so you can have two or more queens. Choosing a knight or rook instead of a queen is called underpromotion.
