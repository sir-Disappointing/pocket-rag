# The basics

## The board
Chess is played on a board of 8 by 8 squares that alternate between light and dark. Place the board so that each player has a light square in the right-hand corner nearest to them. The columns are called files (a to h) and the rows are called ranks (1 to 8).

## Setting up
White's pieces start on ranks 1 and 2, Black's on ranks 7 and 8. The second rank holds eight pawns. On the back rank, from the corners inward: rooks, knights, bishops. The queen goes on the square of her own colour (white queen on d1, black queen on d8), and the king goes next to her.

## Who moves first
White always moves first. After that the players take turns, one move each. You may not skip a turn, even if every possible move is bad for you.

## The goal
You win by checkmating the opponent's king: the king is attacked (in check) and there is no legal move that gets it out of check. A game can also end when a player resigns, runs out of time, or in a draw.

## Check
A king that is attacked is in check. You must get out of check on your very next move, by moving the king, capturing the attacking piece, or putting a piece in between. You may never make a move that leaves or puts your own king in check.
