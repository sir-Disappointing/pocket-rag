# Quick facts
- Board: 8 by 8, light square in each player's right-hand corner. White moves first.
- Values (rough guide): pawn 1, knight 3, bishop 3, rook 5, queen 9. The king is priceless.
- Ways to win: checkmate, resignation, opponent's time runs out. Ways to draw: stalemate, agreement, threefold repetition, fifty-move rule, insufficient material.
