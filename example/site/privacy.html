Privacy

This site has no accounts, cookies or analytics.

Only when you use the assistant is data sent out: your question, the last messages of the conversation and the parts of this site that match your question go to Anthropic (USA), which makes Claude. With your own API key this goes straight from your browser to Anthropic, and the key stays in your browser. If the site uses a proxy, the proxy sees your IP address to limit the number of questions per minute, and doesn't store the conversation.

Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API conversations are not used for training by default. See Anthropic's privacy policy for current details. Don't type personal data into the assistant.
